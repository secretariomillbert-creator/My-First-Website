FAQ's

A few things you might be wondering.

Quick answers about my services, process, and the kinds of work shown in this portfolio.

What services do you offer?

I work on UI/UX design, responsive front-end development, and interfaces for dashboards, HR systems, and information portals.

What kinds of projects do you work on?

The portfolio highlights administrative systems, HR workflows, digital galleries, and web portals. Share your goals and I can discuss whether the project is a good fit.

What does your process look like?

We start by clarifying the audience, goals, and scope. Then I plan the user flows, design the interface, build the responsive experience, and refine it through review.

Which tools and technologies do you use?

For web work, I use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, with Figma for interface design. The right tools depend on the project requirements.

Can you work with an existing design or project?

Yes. I can help refine an existing interface or continue a project, depending on its current state and the work needed.

How can I get in touch?

Use “Let’s talk,” “Learn more,” or “Ask about a project” to open the contact form. Submitting the form sends your message to my email through Web3Forms.
